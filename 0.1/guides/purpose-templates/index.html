    Not legal advice

    dpdpkit is software that helps you implement obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025. It does not provide legal advice and does not guarantee compliance.

    Industry purpose templates¶

    dpdpkit init writes a two-purpose starter file. For common sectors there are fuller starting points: a complete dpdpkit.yaml with the data items, processors, purposes, retention rules and an itemised notice (English and Hindi) that a typical business in that sector begins with.

    dpdpkit init --list-templates
dpdpkit init --template edtech

    To correct a template with a client, open it as a data-mapping workbook (dpdpkit workbook new --template edtech) and import the result back into dpdpkit.yaml.

    Template For What to look at first
    edtech Learning apps, coaching and test prep, school and LMS platforms Learners under 18: parental consent, no tracking or targeted ads
    health Clinics, telemedicine, diagnostics, health apps Health data marked sensitive; medical-record retention; s7_f emergency purpose
    ecommerce D2C brands, marketplaces, online stores Invoices kept under tax law (s7_d); the Third Schedule switch for 2 crore+ users
    fintech Payments, lending, wallets, distribution apps KYC and transaction records kept under law (s7_d); masked Aadhaar only
    saas B2B software Covers your own users and leads, not the customer data you process for clients

    A starting point, not legal advice

    Every purpose, legal basis, retention period and line of notice wording in a template is an assumption for you and your counsel to confirm. Comments at the top of each file list the questions to settle. The Hindi text should be reviewed by a native speaker before you publish it. The notice wording in each template goes through a lawyer review; dpdpkit init --list-templates shows whether it has been reviewed yet, and Notice review explains how to get your own notice approved.

    How the templates are built¶

    Every template follows the same rules, and the test suite in dpdpkit-core checks them:

    • Nothing is pre-ticked. Purposes the service cannot run without are required: true; everything else is optional consent.
    • Optional consent ends at withdrawal. Every optional purpose uses retention: {trigger: withdrawal}, so withdrawing consent schedules erasure. Records the law makes you keep belong to a required purpose or to one with a Section 7 legal basis.
    • Legal retention uses a Section 7 basis. KYC records, transaction records and tax invoices use legal_basis: s7_d, so an erasure request leaves that data in place (see Retention and erasure).
    • Every purpose has a retention rule, every data item and processor is used by at least one purpose, and every purpose and data item has its Hindi text, so the Hindi notice never falls back to English.
    • Every notice passes the automated checks of dpdpkit notice check (example names and links aside).
    • Sector overlays are named, not enabled. education, health-abdm and rbi-kyc are planned for v0.5; the templates say where to add them once they ship.

    Sector notes¶

    Ed-tech. DPDP Act s.9 and Rule 10 need verifiable consent from a parent or guardian for learners under 18, and bar tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children. The template limits product_improvement and marketing to adult learners and parents. Until the guardian flow ships (v0.5), verify the parent in your own sign-up flow and record their choices with kit.consent.grant().

    Health. Medical-record retention comes from medical and state law, not the DPDP Rules, so the periods are placeholders. Pharmacies and labs you share data with are usually Data Fiduciaries in their own right; the template names them in purpose descriptions rather than listing them as processors.

    E-commerce. If you have at least 2 crore registered users in India, Rule 8(1) and the Third Schedule set a 3-year inactivity erasure. Swap the account purpose's retention for the commented line:

    retention: {third_schedule_class: ecommerce}

    The period and its start date then come from the policy pack.

    Fintech. Mark kyc and transactions complete with kit.retention.purpose_completed() when the customer leaves; the retention clock for the legally required period starts then. Whether credit-bureau reporting rests on consent or on a legal obligation is flagged for counsel.

    SaaS. For the data your customers put into the product you are usually their Data Processor, governed by your contract with them. The template covers what you hold as a Data Fiduciary: user accounts, billing, support, product analytics and marketing.

    From Python¶

    Onboarding tools can read the same templates:

    from dpdpkit import InMemoryRepository, kit_from_config
from dpdpkit.templates import list_templates, load_template

for template in list_templates():
    print(template.id, template.title, template.summary)

config = load_template("health").config()  # a dict, ready to edit
kit = kit_from_config(config, InMemoryRepository(), signing_key="change-me")
